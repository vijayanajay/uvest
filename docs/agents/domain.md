## 1. Domain Glossary (Indian Market & Quant Terminology)

### Asset Classes & Instruments
- **Equity (Cash / CNC / MIS):** Shares of NSE/BSE listed companies traded for immediate delivery (T+1 rolling settlement) or intraday leverage.
- **Futures (Stock Futures / FUT):** Standardized, leveraged derivative contracts settled on the last Thursday of the expiry month. Governed by lot sizes (e.g., RELIANCE 250 shares) and exchange margin requirements (~20–25% SPAN + Exposure).
- **Options (CE / PE):** **STRICTLY PROHIBITED IN UVEST.** Non-linear derivatives with theta decay and unbounded tail risk. Excluded by architectural decree.

### Indian Market Microstructure & Mechanics
- **Bhavcopy:** Daily official trade summary published by the National Stock Exchange (NSE) post-market, containing symbol-level open, high, low, close, volume, delivery quantity, and turnover.
- **Delivery Volume %:** Percentage of total daily traded volume that resulted in actual transfer of shares between demat accounts rather than intraday day-trading. $\text{Delivery \%} = \frac{\text{Delivery Quantity}}{\text{Traded Quantity}} \times 100$. High delivery ($>50\%$) on rising price indicates institutional accumulation.
- **Open Interest (OI):** Total number of outstanding, active futures contracts not yet settled or closed. Rising OI + Rising Price = Long Buildup; Falling OI + Rising Price = Short Covering; Rising OI + Falling Price = Short Buildup.
- **MWPL (Market Wide Position Limit) & F&O Ban:** Total open interest across all brokers cannot exceed 95% of a stock's free-float limit set by SEBI. Stocks reaching $>95\%$ enter the F&O Ban Period where no new futures positions are permitted.
- **ASM / GSM (Surveillance Measures):** Additional / Graded Surveillance Measures imposed by SEBI on stocks exhibiting abnormal volatility, promoter debt, or price manipulation.
- **Futures Basis:** The price difference between the active Futures contract and the Underlying Cash Spot price ($\text{Basis} = P_{\text{FUT}} - P_{\text{Spot}}$). Indicates carry cost / financing demand.

### Quantitative Analytics
- **Dynamic Time Warping (DTW):** Algorithm measuring geometric similarity between two temporal sequences of differing speeds. Uses Sakoe-Chiba constraint band on Z-score normalized closing prices to identify historical multi-year analogs.
- **Purged Group Time-Series Cross-Validation:** ML cross-validation scheme where training and testing sets are strictly time-ordered, groups of overlapping forward labels are purged, and a 5-day embargo period prevents autocorrelation leakage.
- **Price Cone ([P10, P50, P90]):** Volatility-projected price distribution over 22 trading days (1 month). P10 represents the 10th percentile bearish boundary; P90 represents the 90th percentile bullish expansion.
- **Max Adverse Excursion (MAE):** The maximum unrealized loss (drawdown) experienced by a trade prior to hitting target or expiry.
- **Max Favorable Excursion (MFE):** The maximum unrealized profit achieved by a trade during its lifespan.
- **Anchored VWAP (AVWAP):** Volume-Weighted Average Price anchored to a specific pivotal event (e.g., the swing low preceding a recommendation), serving as institutional benchmark support.

### Fundamental & Governance Metrics
- **Piotroski F-Score:** A 9-point composite score evaluating financial quality across profitability, leverage/liquidity, and operating efficiency.
- **Altman Z''-Score:** Insolvency prediction model adapted for emerging market manufacturing and service corporations.
- **Promoter Pledging:** Percentage of promoter equity pledged as collateral to NBFCs/banks to raise debt. High pledging ($>15\%$) poses severe liquidation margin call risks; $>25\%$ is a hard system kill switch.
- **CFO-to-PAT Ratio:** Operating Cash Flow divided by Net Profit After Tax. Tests whether reported paper earnings are supported by real cash collections.

### Benchmark Curation & Hazard Evidence
- **Level Sourcing (Pledge):** The rule that a `pledge_hazard` row's `promoter_pledge_pct` must be pinned to a point-in-time NSE shareholding-pattern disclosure (the value actually disclosed as of `T_rec`), never an authored number chosen to fill a census cell. Governs how any new pledge row evidences its breaker value.
- **Window Pinning (Earnings):** The rule that an `earnings_hazard` row's `sessions_to_earnings` must be the trading-session count from `T_rec` to the issuer's actual announced results date, never an authored default. Two existing rows (SCEN-014, SCEN-017) carry the class's authored 2-session default rather than a documented calendar fact.
- **Boundary Probe:** A hazard row whose breaker measurement sits near the decision boundary (a marginal ≈26% pledge; earnings at 1 or 6 sessions) so the rule-exercise census tests whether the breaker fires *at* its threshold, not only comfortably past it. Contrast with a mid-band row, which adds a census cell without probing the boundary.

## 2. Architectural Decision Records (ADRs)

### ADR-001: Strict Exclusion of Options
- **Status:** APPROVED & FROZEN
- **Context:** Univest occasionally offers options tips. Option buying suffers from severe time decay (Theta) and IV collapse; option selling carries asymmetric tail risk.
- **Decision:** Reject any option contract at ingestion. Only Equities and Futures are permitted.
- **Enforcement (2026-10-13):** "Only Equities and Futures are permitted" is enforced as a **closed domain**, not as a blacklist. Stage 0 accepts exactly `CASH` and `FUT` and refuses anything else with `ERR_INSTRUMENT_UNRECOGNIZED` (exit 2, no canonical payload), because a value the gate cannot classify is a value downstream stages *will* classify — as cash. This is what stops an option written with look-alike letters (`Type: СЕ`, Cyrillic) from entering the pipeline as an "unknown instrument": it is not the ASCII token `CE`, so the token gate cannot name it, and before the closed domain it was scored as an equity. `FUTURES` is deliberately not an alias: stages 1/5/9 key off `FUT` alone, so accepting the long form would hand them a futures trade they read as cash. The rejection is reported with its own code, never as `ERR_OPTIONS_PROHIBITED` — a paste that is not a classified option must not inflate the options rejection rate.

### ADR-002: Local-First Zero-Cloud Architecture
- **Status:** APPROVED & FROZEN
- **Context:** Cloud microservices, hosted databases, and external SaaS introduce operational failure modes, token leaks, and latency.
- **Decision:** Python 3.11+ scripts, local SQLite databases (`uvest_history.db`, `dhan_cache.db`), flat JSON standard input/output contracts.

### ADR-003: Tiered Data Hierarchy (Free First, Dhan Fallback)
- **Status:** APPROVED & FROZEN
- **Context:** Commercial API rate limits and paid subscriptions should be minimized.
- **Decision:** Free web scrapers (Bhavcopy, Screener.in, yfinance) are the primary source. Dhan API is queried strictly for 5m/10m candles and real-time order depth when public data lacks resolution.

### ADR-004: Spec & Evals Frozen Before TDD
- **Status:** APPROVED & FROZEN
- **Context:** Developing quant features without frozen mathematical seams causes regressions and "vibe coding."
- **Decision:** Complete specification in `BRD.md`, tickets in `docs/tickets/`, and behavioral evals in `docs/agents/evals.md` must be frozen before implementation starts.

### ADR-005: Retention of Hard Safety Circuit Breakers (Rejecting Soft Pledge Pricing)
- **Status:** APPROVED & FROZEN
- **Context:** An alternative proposal in `docs/understanding.md` suggested softening circuit breakers (specifically Promoter Pledging > 25%) by attempting to "price the risk" into a continuous expected value calculation rather than hard-rejecting the trade.
- **Decision:** REJECT the softening proposal. In Indian equities and futures, promoter pledge crises trigger cascading NBFC/bank margin call liquidations and lower circuits where bid liquidity completely evaporates. A retail trader physically cannot exit at the theoretical stop loss in cash, while in futures, broker RMS executes fire-sale liquidations. Hard circuit breakers (Promoter Pledge > 25%, Inverted R:R < 1.0, Options, F&O Ban, Futures Earnings < 7d) remain absolute vetoes (`AVOID`).

### ADR-006: 7-State Verdict Schema & Daily Stewardship Contract
- **Status:** APPROVED & FROZEN
- **Context:** The original tri-state verdict (`GO`, `CONDITIONAL_GO`, `NO_GO`) was too coarse, and the system lacked lifecycle ownership of active trades after entry.
- **Decision:** Adopt the 7-state lifecycle vocabulary: Pre-entry (`GO`, `GO_WEAK`, `WAIT`, `AVOID`) and Post-entry (`HOLD`, `PRESSURE`, `EXIT`). Every non-GO verdict carries a mandatory `what_would_flip_me`. Confirmed trades enter daily stewardship where `still_chance_pct` is computed deterministically from premise evidence and published in a canonical daily report card.

### ADR-008: Hazard-Census Growth via Pinned Boundary Probes, Not Authored Levels
- **Status:** APPROVED (sourcing rules) — catalog growth remains a separate owner command; `business_test_cases.md` is untouched by this decision.
- **Context:** Gate 2's rule-exercise census counts distinct tripped `(arm, value)` pairs, so `pledge_hazard` (5) and `earnings_hazard` (2) are capped by the distinct hazard values the frozen catalog documents — a ceiling no number of extra rows can raise. A proposal to raise these by adding rows with authored levels (a disclosed value above the 25% floor, or the class's default session window) was grilled, which falsified two of its assumptions: (1) every seeded hazard row is a full point-in-time scenario carrying a pinned `ohlcv_prefix.json`/`forward_tape.json` whose forward motion must corroborate the catalog outcome — not a stage-6-only breaker probe; (2) two of the five earnings windows (SCEN-014, SCEN-017) are already authored defaults, not documented calendar facts, so authoring more windows would deepen the very weakness the census measures. Separately, `pledge_hazard`'s five levels ({32.0, 34.7, 54.2, 62.5, 99.0}) all sit comfortably over the 25% kill switch — none probes the boundary.
- **Decision:** Any future hazard-row growth must (a) pin each new `promoter_pledge_pct` to a real point-in-time NSE shareholding-pattern disclosure and each new `sessions_to_earnings` to the issuer's actual announced results date — never an authored number or an authored default window; (b) scope to boundary probes that test each breaker at its threshold (a marginal ≈26% pledge, earnings at 1 and 6 sessions), so the census rises as a byproduct of decision-boundary coverage rather than as the goal; and (c) carry the full pinned-tape footprint every existing hazard row already carries. A census rise is not, by itself, sufficient justification: mid-band rows that only inflate the count are refused, and absent an owner command to grow the catalog, the honest position is that pledge 5 / earnings 2 remain the complete, correct measurements of the frozen catalog as written.

### ADR-009: Hazard Boundary Probes — Pinned Evidence at the Breaker Thresholds
- **Status:** APPROVED & FROZEN
- **Context:** The stage-6 hazard classes' rule-exercise census is capped by the distinct hazard values their rows carry (`pledge_hazard` 5, `earnings_hazard` 2), and neither value is a function of the pinned tape — catalog growth is the only honest lever (ADR-008). The pre-grill full package (+7 rows) was rejected in favor of rows that probe each breaker at its threshold; the refusal-capable sourcing proof then found no shareholding-pattern disclosure in the 25.1–28.0% band, so the marginal-pledge probe could not be sourced as designed.
- **Decision:** Ship exactly two boundary-probe scenarios — earnings at 1 trading session (`SCEN-062`) and 6 sessions (`SCEN-063`) — each with its hazard value pinned to the issuer's actual announced NSE results date per ADR-008, each a full pinned-tape scenario realizing its documented `LOSS` under the frozen first-touch resolution, each backed by recorded stage-6 bytes. `SCEN-061` (marginal pledge) is dropped and disclosed under the memo's refusal rule — never authored to complete a table; widening the band to a sourced 30.5% disclosure was considered and declined as an amendment to ADR-008 made only to raise a count. Quotas move `earnings_hazard` 5→7 and total 50→52 in the same change; the census floor re-freezes 38→40 (V12). Sourcing evidence: `docs/agents/adr-009-hazard-boundary-probes.md`, addendum 2026-10-13.

### ADR-010: Stage-7 Regime Evidence — One Fixture, One Waiver, Spec Vocabulary
- **Status:** APPROVED & FROZEN (owner command, 2026-10-14)
- **Context:** Stage 7 (TICK-007) was frozen but unimplemented, and three contract seams were ambiguous or in conflict *before* code existed: (1) TICK-007 froze `nifty_trend` as `ABOVE_50_200_DMA` / `BELOW_50_ABOVE_200` / `BELOW_200_DMA` while shipped stage-9 code scored `BULLISH` / `NEUTRAL` / `BEARISH` — tokens no producer can emit, so every real run would silently take the 0.2 default; (2) TICK-007 asked for a 4-quadrant RRG from the MRS *level* alone, which has no second axis; (3) one `waived` flag covered five independent datasets with no rule for a partial gap, and stage 9's `score_regime` defaults a missing component to a mid value — data absence beating a genuinely weak reading is exactly the inflation BRD §10.4 forbids. The evidence source itself (recorded index series) had no schema, while stage 4's pending 20D NIFTY beta and sectoral-correlation features need the same series.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Spec outranks shipped code (ADR-004):** the `nifty_trend` vocabulary is TICK-007's, now pinned in BRD §7.2 as the single source; stage 9's `score_regime` and its test fixture adopt the frozen tokens with the same numeric calibration (0.3 / 0.2 / 0.0). When frozen spec and shipped code disagree, the code moves.
  2. **RRG gets its missing axis:** `sector_mrs_mom` = $\text{MRS}_t - \text{MRS}_{t-5}$ (5-session change, percentage points) joins the contract (BRD §7.1, §9 inventory row 7); the quadrant map and its zero boundary are frozen in TICK-007.
  3. **One fixture, one waiver:** the stage runs entirely from a single `--regime-fixture` bundle; any absent, short, malformed or misaligned input — including a symbol absent from the sector map — waives the **whole** payload (`waived: true`, every metric `null`, `missing_inputs` naming each cause, exit 0). BRD §10.4's waiver rule is payload-granular: stage 9 scores `out_7_regime.json` as one artifact, so component-level waiver arithmetic would require a stage-9 scoring seam that does not exist, and a mid-value default for one missing component is the inflation §10.4 forbids. Partial (per-component) waiver was considered and explicitly rejected until such a seam is specced.
  4. **Evidence schema `uvest-market-context/1`:** one JSON bundle whose series are keyed by Yahoo-style tickers (`^NSEI`, `^INDIAVIX`, `^CNXENERGY`, …) plus the `NSE500_BREADTH` and `FII_DII_NET` aggregates, each carrying `unit` and `values: [{date, value}]`, with a mandatory `provenance` block disclosing `origin: recorded | authored`. Stage 7 is its first consumer; stage 4 reads the same bundle instead of growing a second fetcher and cache — the no-lookahead truncation at T_rec then travels with one bundle, not three. The bundle can be authored (seeded, disclosed) or recorded live: `scripts/fetch_market_context.py` is the one live fetcher (tier-1 sources; a failed source omits its series and stage 7 waives naming it, never an invented value). *Honoured 2026-10-14: stage 4 (`scripts/4_ml_direction.py`) takes that same artifact as `--context-fixture`, and the runner fetches live context **once**, in front of the first consumer, handing the one bundle to both — the 20-session beta and sectoral correlation are read from it rather than from a second cache, and the no-lookahead truncation at T_rec travels with a single file. Stage 4's own weights (`models/lgbm_direction_v1.*`) are a disclosed authored stand-in: BRD §5.2's NSE-F&O-universe model remains the open BRD §8 item.* *Superseded 2026-10-14: the stand-in is retired and the shipped weights are trained from the sourced corpus (ADR-011).*
  5. **Sector map lives in code:** symbol → sectoral-index mapping is the module constant `SECTOR_INDEX_MAP` in `scripts/7_sector_macro_regime.py` (the stage-0 `OPTIONS_TYPES` precedent: domain data a test pins, no new file, no I/O). An unmapped symbol waives; it never guesses an index. Extract to shared data only when a second consumer exists (stage 4). *Executed 2026-10-14: stage 4 is that second consumer, so the map now lives in `scripts/_sector_map.py` and both stages import the same constant — a pin added for one is a pin for both, and neither resolves an index by guessing.*
  6. **Macro signals size, they do not veto** (second owner command, 2026-10-14): stage 9 consumes `breadth_regime: RISK_OFF` (BRD §7.2 “all long setups gated or reduced”) and `fii_dii_flow_bias: NET_SELLING` (BRD §7.4 “cautious sizing”) through the existing BRD §6.4 size-cap lever — either signal caps a would-be `GO` at `GO_WEAK` (50% size) with `confidence_deficit` naming each source; both together cap at the same 50% and never compound. A waived or absent `out_7_regime.json` caps nothing (the waiver rule). The fatal breaker stays exactly BRD §10.3 #6 (VIX $> 24$ **and** breadth $< 30$): a Risk-Off breadth print alone is *priced*, never a veto, so the frozen six-breaker registry (ADR-005) is untouched. §7.3's Elevated-VIX 50%-sizing guidance is deliberately left unwired — extending the same cap lever to `vix_regime: ELEVATED` is a separate owner decision.

### ADR-011: Sourced Training Corpus, Purged Group CV, and a Stated Direction Event
- **Context:** BRD §5.2 asks for a LightGBM booster trained on the NSE F&O universe with purged group time-series CV and a calibrated Brier score. What shipped was a disclosed authored stand-in (one stock, one authored market window), which left eval dimensions 4–5 `[SKIP]`: grading a stand-in's Brier score as the §5.2 model's would be the unbacked claim `evals.md` §2 forbids. The corpus that could replace it already existed — 173 committed `SYMBOL_YYYYMM.json` vintages of real yfinance daily bars — but the *market* half stage 4's two cross-asset features read did not, and the only `uvest-market-context/1` bundle in the repo is one authored 2026 window.
- **Decision:**
  1. **The market side is a committed sourced corpus too.** `tests/evals/build_index_corpus.py` pulls the panel (through `fetch_market_context.fetch_yahoo_closes` — one fetch implementation, not two) and commits `tests/fixtures/history/indices_daily.json`. Training is then offline and reproducible from committed bytes. Series the sources do **not** serve historically are recorded under `absent` with the reasons they gave — never a stub, never a proxy index — and the trained universe is consequently the subset of the F&O universe whose sectoral index is served (`corpus.symbols_excluded` names the rest, per symbol, in the artifact itself). *Extended 2026-10-15 by decision 12: the panel is served by two tier-1 sources, and each series says which one served it.*
  2. **One feature implementation, one mixture implementation.** The trainer imports `scripts/4_ml_direction.py`'s own `feature_vector` and its cone primitives (`_pooled`, `mixture_quantile`), so a feature or a cone cannot mean one thing in training and another at inference — the same reason ADR-010 decision 4 gave for one bundle.
  3. **Rows are point-in-time in both directions.** A row's market and sector windows are truncated at its own session, and a row whose date has no index session of its own is skipped rather than stamped with a stale market window. Both halves are pinned by equivalence in `tests/test_train_direction_model.py` (same corpus cut short of a synthetic regime change ⇒ identical features for the rows that survive), with a vacuity guard proving the regime change was real.
  4. **The split's frozen reading.** Folds are contiguous chronological blocks of the unique row sessions. Purging is per symbol and keyed on the fold's **date span**, measured on that symbol's own session calendar: a training row goes when its 22-session label window would reach the span, or when it sits within the 5-session embargo tail (BRD §5.2). Keying the purge on the sessions a symbol happens to have *inside* the span — the obvious implementation — keeps the row directly before a fold that a halted symbol never traded in, whose label window covers the whole fold; that case is a named test. Every row is tested exactly once, folds are ordered and disjoint, and the counts are written into the artifact.
  5. **The engine states the event the eval grades.** `direction_probability` = $P(\text{BULL}) + \varphi \cdot P(\text{NEUTRAL})$ joins the payload, with $\varphi$ (`neutral_up_share`) measured from the corpus — the share of NEUTRAL-labelled training rows that closed above their entry — and frozen in the calibration. A three-class model does not answer a yes/no direction question by itself, and `prob_target_hit_22d` (the target-versus-stop race) is a different event; defaulting $\varphi$ to a coin flip would silently mis-grade dimension 4, so a calibration without it waives.
  6. **Replaceability is a re-run, not an edit.** The artifact path is the contract; re-running the trainer after the corpus grows rewrites booster and calibration together, and training twice produces identical bytes (asserted). The authored stand-in and its builder were retired in the same change rather than kept as a second way to produce one artifact.
  7. **The cone is the path envelope the gate grades** (owner command, 2026-10-14): `price_cone_p10_p50_p90` is re-frozen as the 22-session envelope of the price **path** — `p10`/`p25` from the class-weighted mixture of observed adverse excursions (`mae_pct`), `p75`/`p90` from the mixture of favourable excursions (`mfe_pct`), `p50` from terminal returns. The previous construction inverted all five quantiles from the *terminal* return mixture, so it answered "how far will the close travel" while evals dimension 5 and BRD §4's Expected Range ask "how far will the price travel" — measured over the corpus, 72.3% of rows kept their close inside that interval and only 49.5% kept their path inside it. Two properties follow from the coupling rather than from an assertion: every observed row satisfies $\text{mae} \le \text{terminal} \le \text{mfe}$ with one shared weight vector, so the envelope (a) contains the interval it replaced and (b) is monotone in level by construction, with `mae_projected`/`mfe_projected` remaining the same mixture's medians. This is a *definition* correction to an engine whose claim and whose grader disagreed — not a threshold move: the 80% coverage floor and the 0.18 Brier ceiling are untouched, and the re-measured gate values are published as they fall.
  8. **A path is its session extremes** (owner command, 2026-10-15): the excursion atoms of decision 7 are the forward sessions' **lows and highs**, not their closes — `mae_pct` = the window's worst low against the entry, `mfe_pct` = its best high, the terminal return from the closing session, and the invariant $\text{mae} \le \text{terminal} \le \text{mfe}$ still holds row by row (a minimum of lows is at or below the last close, a maximum of highs at or above it), so decision 7's monotonicity argument carries over unchanged. Decision 7 fixed *which* mixture each level inverts; it left the realization of the atoms unstated, and the first implementation measured the closes — a strictly narrower quantity than the bars the coverage gate grades, so the envelope was again answering a question one step away from its grader's. The per-symbol corpus vintages carry `open/high/low/close`, so the atoms come from the same committed, sourced rows: no new source, no authored widening. Features stay close-based (`feature_vector` reads closes; the index panel is close-only), so this moves the level of the envelope and the projected excursions, never the direction model's inputs. Measured before and after over the same corpus rows, together with the gate's own reading, in `docs/agents/evals.md` §2.
  9. **The coverage gate grades the window its pools came from** (owner command, 2026-10-15): evals dimension 5's denominator is the cone-labeled records whose pinned forward tape lies **entirely** inside `provenance.corpus.row_window` — the artifact's own disclosure of the sessions that trained the booster (`run_evals.trained_corpus_window`, so a retrain moves the scope instead of a stale constant beside the gate) — and a record outside it is **disclosed on the scorecard with its span, excluded from the denominator, and never counted as a miss**. `dataset_refs.forward_tape_span` reads the same tape file the frozen `$derive` rules read, so the span used to scope a row cannot disagree with the numbers that row is graded from; the rule is symmetric, so an out-of-window record can neither prop up nor drag down the percentage. On this benchmark two Covid tapes (2020-03-24..2020-04-28, 2020-03-25..2020-04-29) fall before the window opens on 2020-04-09 and are named ungraded; the four in-window records all still miss, so the verdict stays **0.0%** — the scoping makes the denominator honest, not kinder. Because the scope decides *what is graded*, it lives in the contract (`docs/agents/evals.md` §3 #5) with the seam `tests/test_run_evals.py::TestConeCoverageScope`. *Superseded the same day by decision 10: the corpus now reaches both Covid tapes (the row window opens 2020-02-18), so all six records grade and this decision's exclusion prints nothing on this benchmark — the rule itself stands as the fallback for any record the corpus genuinely cannot reach.*
  10. **The corpus reaches the tapes it is graded on** (owner command, 2026-10-15): decision 9 turned `row_window` into the gate's denominator, which surfaced the corpus's own reach gap as a would-be *permanent* exclusion — the two Covid tapes (SCEN-001/SCEN-002, from 2020-03-24) sat outside the window not because no stock vintage reaches 2020 (RELIANCE and SBIN vintages run 2019-04-30 → 2020-04-30) but because the *index panel* did not: a row needs `CROSS_ASSET_WINDOW + 1` index sessions at or before its own date (`4_ml_direction.common_return_windows`), so the panel's first session (2020-03-09) — not the stocks' — is what opened `row_window` on 2020-04-09, weeks after SBIN's own 200-session floor (2020-02-18). The panel fetch window is therefore **derived from the span of the committed stock corpus it trains beside** (`build_index_corpus.fetch_sessions`, replacing a fixed session count whose comment had gone stale at “2021-05 onward” while the vintages reached 2018-03), pulled from the same tier-1 source through the same fetcher and committed as before; the stocks, not the panel, then set where the window opens. Decision 9's disclosure rule is untouched — it still governs any record the corpus genuinely cannot reach — but a reach gap is a defect of two files (panel span vs benchmark tapes), so the seams read both: `tests/test_build_index_corpus.py` (the committed panel covers the committed stock corpus's span), `tests/test_train_direction_model.py` (rows build at or before the benchmark's earliest cone tape from the committed corpus alone), and `tests/test_run_evals.py::TestConeCoverageScope` (both Covid tapes graded end-to-end, with the disclosure seam unchanged for the synthetic out-of-window case). What the re-measured envelope and gate then say is published in `docs/agents/evals.md` §2 as it falls — the 80% floor and the 0.18 ceiling do not move.
  11. **A forward label window is one vintage deep** (owner command, 2026-10-15). The corpus is per-symbol-month vintages and they are not consecutive: SBIN's and RELIANCE's run `202003` and then `202204`, so the merged series steps from 2020-03-31 to 2021-05-03. Reading a 22-session forward window off that merged series labeled the rows on the near side of the jump with excursions from fourteen months later, and those atoms went into the cone's pools as a one-month move. A row is now built only when **one committed vintage contains its own session and every session after it** (`label_window_dates`), so no window can straddle two fetches however the merged dates happen to line up; the rest are dropped and counted in `provenance.corpus.label_window` (44 rows on this corpus: the `202003` tails of RELIANCE and SBIN). This is a data-contract property, not a modeling one: an excursion the corpus cannot vouch for is not evidence about a month, and the two tapes' deepest pooled atoms were exactly these rows. Seam: `tests/test_train_direction_model.py::TestLabelWindowsStayInsideAVintage`.
  12. **The panel has a second tier-1 source, for the sectors the first does not serve** (owner command, 2026-10-15). Yahoo returned a single session for `^CNXAUTO`, `^CNXFMCG`, `^CNXMETAL`, `^CNXINFRA`, `^CNXENERGY` and `^CNXBANK` (and no such symbol at all for the last), which kept RELIANCE, LT, ADANIPORTS, MARUTI, ITC, TATASTEEL and the PSU banks out of the trained universe entirely — including three of the six sectors the benchmark's own engine-graded rows trade in. NSE's daily index snapshot (`ind_close_all_DDMMYYYY.csv`, the official `Open/High/Low/Close` of every Nifty sector index, published per trading day) carries all of them back through the corpus, and it is the free-first tier BRD §5.2 and AGENTS.md §5 already prefer over any aggregator. The fallback is asked **only** about the tickers the first source could not serve with enough sessions to carry a training row, so it cannot quietly become the primary; every served series records its source (`tickers.<ticker>.source`), and the archive's own coverage is counted in `provenance.archive` (`published`, `no_file`). **A day the archive could not be reached is not a day it does not publish**: only HTTP 404 (on both hosts) is recorded as the source's own gap, any other failure raises `ArchiveUnreachable`, names the day, and is never cached — otherwise a network blip would silently shorten a series and read as the source's limit. Measured 2026-10-15: **12 of 12 panel series served, 0 absent** (was 5 served / 7 absent), window 2017-11-30 – 2026-10-15, the archive carrying 2,189 of 2,315 weekdays (126 days it does not publish). Seams: `tests/test_build_index_corpus.py::TestTheFallbackSourceServesWhatYahooDoesNot`, `::TestArchiveDayFetchedHonestly`.
  13. **The cone's pools are the trade's own sector's, when they are deep enough** (owner command, 2026-10-15).   14. **A feature history is one gapless run deep** (owner command, 2026-10-16). Decision 11 made each *label* window sit inside one vintage; the *features* were still read off the merged series, so a row behind SBIN's and RELIANCE's `202003 → 202204` step computed its 200-session history — `sma200_dist`, `vol_ratio_14_60`, the cross-asset windows — over a fourteen-month hole, on exactly the crash-era rows the envelopes lean on.
