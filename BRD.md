## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

Retail advisory applications (such as Univest) publish high-velocity trade recommendations for Indian equities and futures. While these recommendations claim enticing reward-to-risk profiles, retail traders routinely suffer catastrophic drawdowns due to:
1. **Inverted Risk-Reward at Market Entry:** Recommendations arrive after prices have already slipped past the viable entry band.
2. **Ignorance of Institutional Market Microstructure:** Entering long during institutional distribution or retail short-covering rallies.
3. **Fundamental Blind Spots:** Recommending stocks burdened by heavy promoter pledging, deteriorating cash flows (accrual anomalies), or impending earnings announcements.
4. **Market Regime Misalignment:** Trading long setups during macro liquidity contractions, high India VIX volatility regimes, or sector-relative weakness.
5. **Lack of Post-Entry Management & Autopsy:** No disciplined rules when a trade moves into negative territory, and zero post-mortem learning when a stop-loss is triggered.

### 1.1 The Solution
**UVEST** is an institutional-grade, agentic trade validation engine operated inside an Agentic IDE (Antigravity, Cursor, Windsurf, VS Code Agent). 
- It ingests Univest recommendations (via raw text or screenshot OCR).
- It executes a suite of lightweight, modular Python tools against **free publicly available data** (NSE India Bhavcopy, Yahoo Finance, Screener.in) and reserves the **Dhan Broker API** as a high-precision layer for intraday candles and order-flow microstructure.
- It synthesizes quantitative, machine-learning, fundamental, and macro regime analytics to deliver **Three Core Predictions**:
  1. **Direction:** 1-Month Swing Trajectory and Probability ($P(\text{Bullish}), P(\text{Neutral}), P(\text{Bearish})$).
  2. **Range:** 1-Month Expected Price Volatility Cone ([P10, P50, P90], Max Adverse Excursion, Max Favorable Excursion).
  3. **Optimal Price Range:** Precise Limit Execution Zone (Anchored VWAP, Liquidity Pool, and Support Retest) vs. Univest’s recommended entry band.
- It generates both a concise markdown terminal report and a **standalone, rich interactive HTML visualization dashboard** for every recommendation.
- It tracks every recommendation in a local SQLite database, guides the user on **daily position management** when trades move in an adverse direction, and executes an automated **Failure Autopsy** upon trade resolution to calibrate future scoring.

### 1.2 Non-Negotiable Scope Boundaries & Guardrails
- **Supported Asset Classes:** **Equities (Cash / CNC / MIS)** and **Futures (NSE Equity Stock Futures)**.
- **Strict Prohibition: NO OPTIONS TRADING EVER.** Option buying suffers from non-linear theta decay and implied volatility crush; option selling introduces unbounded tail risk incompatible with this system's risk framework.
- **The Data Hierarchy:** Free public data first (Bhavcopy, NSE web endpoints, yfinance, Screener). Dhan API is queried strictly as a precision fallback for intraday resolution (5m, 10m) or when public data is missing or stale.
- **Zero Hallucination:** Every number, indicator, win rate, and ratio displayed to the user must trace directly to a local JSON output generated by an executed script.
- **YAGNI & Boring Technology:** Python 3.11+, standard library, SQLite3, lightweight math/ML (numpy, scipy, fastdtw, lightgbm), and local flat JSON contracts. No microservice bloat, no distributed queues, no heavy external databases.

## 5. Quantitative Analytics Engine (Top 1% Hedge-Fund Edge)

### 5.1 Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) Pattern Matcher (`scripts/3_dtw_pattern.py`)
- **Objective:** Screen the past 5–10 years of NSE daily price action (1,250–2,500 trading days) to discover historical periods where the target stock exhibited geometric price and volume trajectories identical to the recent 30-day window.
- **Mathematical Procedure:**
  1. Extract the stock's closing prices for the last $N = 30$ trading days: $X = [x_1, x_2, \dots, x_N]$.
  2. Normalize $X$ using Z-score standardization ($z_i = \frac{x_i - \mu_X}{\sigma_X}$) to achieve scale invariance.
  3. Slide a 30-day window across the 10-year historical series $Y$, Z-score normalize each candidate window $Y_k$.
  4. Compute the Sakoe-Chiba constrained DTW distance $D(X, Y_k)$ using `fastdtw`.
  5. Select the top $K = 15$ nearest historical analogs (lowest DTW distance).
  6. Extract the subsequent 22-trading-day (1-month forward) performance of these 15 analogs.
- **Output Metrics:**
  - `dtw_forward_win_rate`: Percentage of analogs that reached a $+4\%$ gain within 22 bars.
  - `dtw_forward_median_return`: Median percentage return over the subsequent 1 month.
  - `dtw_forward_max_drawdown`: Median maximum adverse excursion (MAE) experienced by matching analogs.

### 5.2 Machine Learning 1-Month Direction Engine (`scripts/4_ml_direction.py`)
- **Model Architecture:** LightGBM Classifier & Regressor trained on historical NSE F&O universe (last 7 years of daily data).
- **Validation Scheme:** Strict **Purged Group Time-Series Cross-Validation** (zero lookahead bias, 5-day embargo period between training and test folds to account for return autocorrelation).
- **Engineered Feature Vector:**
  - *Technical Drift:* 10-day, 20-day, 50-day EMA slopes; 14-day RSI; MACD histogram normalized by ATR; ADX trend strength.
  - *Volatility Spectrum:* 14-day Parkinson Volatility, Garman-Klass Volatility, ATR/Price ratio, Bollinger Bandwidth.
  - *Mean Reversion & Gap:* Distance from 20-day SMA in standard deviations (Z-score); 5-day cumulative overnight gap returns.
  - *Microstructure Momentum:* 5-day slope of Delivery Volume %; Volume-Weighted Price Drive (VWPD).
  - *Cross-Asset Correlation:* 20-day rolling beta to NIFTY 50; 20-day rolling correlation to sectoral index.
- **Target Variable:** Binary classification ($1$ if trade reaches $+5\%$ target before hitting $-2.5\%$ stop loss over 22 trading days, $0$ otherwise).
- **Output:** Predicted win probability $P(\text{Success})$ with calibrated Brier score and feature attribution (SHAP values).

### 5.3 Institutional Footprint: Delivery Volume & F&O Open Interest Matrix (`scripts/5_institutional_flow.py`)
In the Indian market, retail traders churn intraday volume while institutional desks accumulate via high delivery percentages and sustained Futures Open Interest buildup.
- **Primary Data Source:** Official NSE Daily Bhavcopy (`sec_bhavdata_full.csv` and `fo_bhav.csv` downloaded automatically via headless scraper).
- **The Holy Trinity Matrix:**

| Price Change | Delivery % vs. 20D Avg | Futures OI Change | Institutional Classification | Action Bias |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **UP** | **High (>50% or +1.5σ)** | **Rising (>+5%)** | **Aggressive Long Buildup** (Institutional Buying) | 🟢 Strong Buy Confirmation |
| **UP** | **Low (<30% or -1.0σ)** | **Falling (<-5%)** | **Retail Short-Covering Trap** (Exhaustion Rally) | 🔴 Reject Buy (Reversal Imminent) |
| **DOWN** | **High (>50% or +1.5σ)** | **Rising (>+5%)** | **Short Buildup** (Institutional Distribution) | 🔴 Reject Buy (Heavy Selling) |
| **DOWN** | **Low (<30% or -1.0σ)** | **Falling (<-5%)** | **Long Unwinding** (Retail Capitulation) | 🟡 Wait for Absorption |

### 5.4 Futures Basis, Rollover & Carrying Cost (For Futures Recommendations)
If the instrument is an NSE Stock Future:
- **Basis Analysis:** Compute Spot-Futures Basis ($\text{Basis} = P_{\text{Futures}} - P_{\text{Spot}}$). An annualized basis $> 7\%$ reflects bullish financing demand; an inverted basis (Futures at a discount to Spot) during an uptrend indicates institutional hedging or aggressive short buildup.
- **Rollover Analysis (Last Week of Month):** If within 5 days of monthly expiry, compute rollover percentage relative to the 3-month average. Rollovers $< 65\%$ indicate institutions are liquidating rather than carrying positions forward.

## 6. Fundamental & Corporate Governance Engine (`scripts/6_fundamental_audit.py`)

No quantitative setup survives an accounting fraud, promoter debt default, or surprise earnings collapse. This engine runs an automated audit across publicly available financial statements (sourced freely via Screener.in API/web interfaces and NSE corporate filings).

### 6.1 Financial Health & Solvency Checkpoints
1. **Piotroski F-Score (Scale 0–9):**
   - Profitability: Positive ROA, positive Operating Cash Flow (CFO), CFO > Net Profit (critical accrual test).
   - Leverage & Liquidity: Lower long-term debt-to-equity ratio YoY, higher Current Ratio YoY, no share dilution.
   - Operating Efficiency: Higher Gross Margin YoY, higher Asset Turnover YoY.
   - *Gate:* $F \le 4 \implies$ Automatic Score Penalty (-15 pts).
2. **Altman Z''-Score (Emerging Market Bankruptcy Formula):**
   - Flags distress risks ($Z < 1.1 \implies$ Distress Zone; $Z > 2.6 \implies$ Safe Zone).
3. **Beneish M-Score (Earnings Manipulation Detection):**
   - Flags aggressive revenue recognition or asset capitalization ($M > -1.78 \implies$ Red Flag).

### 6.2 Cash Flow Quality & Accrual Anomaly
- **CFO-to-PAT Ratio:** If Reported Profit After Tax (PAT) is growing rapidly while Cash Flow from Operations (CFO) is flat or negative for 2 consecutive years ($\text{CFO} / \text{PAT} < 0.6$), the earnings are paper profits driven by uncollected receivables.

### 6.3 Indian Corporate Governance Red Flags (Crucial Kill Switches)
1. **Promoter Pledging (The Indian Circuit Breaker):**
   - **Hard Rule:** If Promoter Pledged Shares $> 15\%$ of total promoter holding, issue severe warning. If $> 25\%$, **INSTANT HARD REJECTION (NO-GO)**. In India, pledged shares trigger lender margin calls and cascading circuit down-moves.
2. **Promoter Holding Trajectory:**
   - Decline in promoter holding $> 2\%$ over the last 2 quarters without OFS/Capex disclosure is flagged as insider selling.
3. **FII & DII Ownership Shifts:**
   - Compares domestic mutual fund and foreign portfolio investor holdings across the last 4 quarters. If retail shareholding is surging while DII/FII shareholding is contracting, the stock is in a classic retail distribution trap.
4. **Auditor Disclosures:**
   - Screen corporate announcements for auditor resignations ("reasons other than routine rotation") within the past 12 months.

### 6.4 Corporate Calendar Hazard Gate
- **Earnings Date Check:** Queries NSE corporate announcements for scheduled board meetings / quarterly earnings releases.
- **Enforcement:** If earnings are scheduled within the trade holding horizon ($< 10$ trading days), **automatic cap at CONDITIONAL GO (50% size)** for Cash Equities, and **HARD NO-GO for Futures** (due to binary gap-down risk that renders stop-losses ineffective).

## 7. Sector, Industry Index & Macro Regime Engine (`scripts/7_sector_macro_regime.py`)

A stock rarely swims against a collapsing sector or macro market tide.

### 7.1 Sectoral Relative Strength (Mansfield RS)
- **Calculation:** Compares the target stock's sector index (e.g., NIFTY BANK, NIFTY IT, NIFTY AUTO, NIFTY METAL) against the benchmark NIFTY 50 over a 50-day rolling window:
  $$\text{MRS}_t = \left( \frac{\text{Sector}_t / \text{NIFTY}_t}{\text{SMA}_{50}(\text{Sector} / \text{NIFTY})} - 1 \right) \times 100$$
- **Stock vs. Sector RS:** Compares the target stock's return trajectory against its parent sectoral index.
- **Relative Rotation Graph (RRG) Quadrants:** Classifies the setup into `LEADING`, `WEAKENING`, `LAGGING`, or `IMPROVING`. Only `LEADING` and `IMPROVING` receive full momentum points.

### 7.2 NIFTY 50 Macro Tape & Market Breadth
- **Macro Trend:** NIFTY 50 relative to its 50-day and 200-day Simple Moving Averages.
- **Market Breadth (NSE 500):** Percentage of NSE 500 stocks trading above their 200-day moving average:
  - $> 65\%$: Risk-On Regime (Bullish aggressive sizing).
  - $40\%–65\%$: Neutral / Selective Stock-Picker Regime.
  - $< 40\%$: Risk-Off Regime (Systemic downtrend; all long setups gated or reduced).

### 7.3 India VIX Regime
- **Low Volatility ($< 12$):** Range compression, slow drift up; breakout setups often fail; buy dips.
- **Normal Volatility ($12 – 18$):** Optimal environment for trend following and swing recommendations.
- **Elevated Volatility ($18 – 24$):** Wide daily swings; requires wider ATR-based stop losses; reduces position sizing by 50%.
- **Panic Volatility ($> 24$):** Macro event or liquidity shock; **HARD AVOID** on new futures long positions.

### 7.4 Institutional Macro Flows (FII / DII Daily Cash Net)
- Rolling 5-day net institutional flow from NSE daily reports. Sustained combined FII + DII net selling ($< -₹5,000 \text{ Cr}$ over 5 days) enforces cautious sizing across all long equity recommendations.

To guarantee high reliability, zero paid subscription overhead, and precision execution, the system implements a strict two-tier data architecture.


### 8.1 Dhan API Client Specifications (`scripts/8_dhan_precision.py`)
- **Authentication:** Consumes `DHAN_CLIENT_ID` and `DHAN_ACCESS_TOKEN` via environment variables (`.env`).
- **Endpoints Utilized:**
  - `POST /v2/charts/historical`: Intraday 5m and 10m historical candle bars for current and previous trading session.
  - `POST /v2/marketfeed/quote`: Real-time market depth to compute Bid-Ask Spread and instantaneous liquidity.
- **Local SQLite Caching (`dhan_cache.db`):** All intraday candle queries are cached locally with a 15-minute Time-To-Live (TTL). Never repeatedly hit Dhan API for the same ticker within the same evaluation window.
- **Graceful Offline Degradation:** If Dhan credentials are missing or the API returns a rate-limit error (HTTP 429), the system marks the intraday precision module as `waived (0 pts)` and completes the validation using daily OHLCV from Tier 1.

## 10. Consolidated Decision Engine & Scoring Model

### 10.1 Multi-Dimensional Scoring Weights (100-Point Model)

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Mathematical Risk-Reward & Slippage Geometry:    20 Pts  │
│ 2. Quant & ML Edge (DTW Match + LightGBM 1M Cone): 25 Pts  │

│ 4. Fundamental Health & Governance Checks:         15 Pts  │
│ 5. Sectoral Relative Strength & Macro VIX Regime:  15 Pts  │
│ 6. Intraday Precision & Execution Zone Confluence:  5 Pts  │
│                                                             │
│ TOTAL AVAILABLE SCORE:                             100 Pts  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 10.2 Hard Disqualifiers (Instant Circuit Breakers $\implies$ Score 0 / Hard REJECT)
If any of the following conditions trigger, the decision engine overrides all scores to **🔴 NO-GO (REJECT)**:
1. **Inverted Risk-Reward ($R:R < 1.0$):** Risking ₹1 to make less than ₹1.
2. **Promoter Pledging $> 25\%$:** Fatal corporate governance hazard.
3. **NSE F&O Ban Period Active:** Trading banned by exchange for the contract.
4. **Earnings Shock Hazard:** Quarterly results announcement within $< 7$ days on a Futures trade.
5. **Severe Volatility Noise Trap:** Recommended Stop-Loss distance is tighter than $0.75 \times \text{Daily ATR}$ (virtually guaranteed to be stopped out by normal intraday noise).
6. **Severe Adverse Regime:** India VIX $> 24$ (Panic Mode) with Market Breadth $< 30\%$ bullish.

### 10.3 Dynamic Scoring Thresholds
- **Effective Score $\ge 80\%$:** 🟢 **GO (EXECUTE FULL SIZE)** — Prime mathematical edge, institutional backing, clean fundamentals.
- **Effective Score $65\% – 79\%$:** 🟡 **CONDITIONAL GO (REDUCE SIZE 50%)** — Viable trade, but requires waiting for a limit pull-back or tighter stop.
- **Effective Score $< 65\%$:** 🔴 **NO-GO (REJECT)** — Negative expectancy; trade dismissed.

*Waiver Rule:* If any data source fails (e.g., Dhan API offline or Screener rate-limited), that check earns 0 points and is marked `waived`. Waived points are subtracted from `max_available_score`. Missing data never inflates and never penalizes the calculated percentage.

## 11. Daily Position Tracking & Adverse Movement Protocol

> **"None of the recommendations considered and opinions given should be lost."**

Once a recommendation is analyzed and accepted by the user, it enters the persistent `uvest_history.db` tracking table. Every morning prior to market open, or post-market close, `scripts/10_daily_triage.py` runs a multi-step check.

### 11.1 The Daily Question: "What should the user do today?"
The system evaluates active trades against live daily bars and classifies the position into one of four states:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                          DAILY POSITION TRIAGE                              │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. PROFIT HARVESTING ZONE (Price within 10% of Target)                      │
│    Action: Trail Stop Loss to Breakeven + 50% of unrealized gains.          │
│                                                                             │
│ 2. HEALTHY RUNWAY ZONE (Price consolidating above Entry)                    │
│    Action: HOLD. Maintain original stop loss. Let mathematical edge work.   │
│                                                                             │
│ 3. ADVERSE MOVEMENT ZONE (Price moving against entry toward Stop Loss)      │
│    Action: Trigger Three-Stage Adverse Movement Protocol (See Section 11.2).│
│                                                                             │
│ 4. STAGNATION / TIME STOP ZONE (Trade active > 12 days without movement)   │
│    Action: FUTURES: Close on Friday to eliminate carry cost. CASH: HOLD.    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 11.2 The Three-Stage Adverse Movement Protocol
When a trade drifts into the negative direction:
- **Level 1 (Drawdown $\le 1.0 \times \text{ATR}$ from Entry): Normal Pullback.**
  - Check: Is institutional delivery continuing to accumulate? Is the sector holding?
  - Verdict: **DO NOTHING. DO NOT AVERAGE DOWN.** Maintain stop-loss discipline.
- **Level 2 (Drawdown $> 1.0 \times \text{ATR}$ or breaks 20 EMA / Structural Support): Warning.**
  - Check: Has the fundamental premise broken? Did FIIs aggressively short the stock in yesterday's Bhavcopy?
  - Verdict: **CANCEL RE-ENTRY ORDERS. TIGHTEN STOP TO PREVIOUS DAY HIGH/LOW.** Prepare for exit.
- **Level 3 (Stop-Loss Hit or Premise Invalidation): Hard Liquidation.**
  - **The Golden Rule:** **NEVER AVERAGE DOWN A LOSING POSITION.** 
  - Action: Execute exit order immediately. Record trade resolution in `uvest_history.db` as `LOSS` or `INVALIDATED`.

## 12. Continuous Learning Machine & Failure Autopsy

When any tracked trade resolves (reaches Target, hits Stop Loss, or expires), `scripts/11_postmortem_learning.py` automatically initiates an autopsy.

### 12.1 The Post-Mortem Failure Autopsy Matrix
Every failed trade (`LOSS`) is systematically decomposed into one of five root causes:
1. `VOLATILITY_NOISE`: Stop hit intraday, but stock closed above stop price by day end. (ATR buffer too tight).
2. `REGIME_SHOCK`: NIFTY crashed $>1.5\%$ or India VIX surged $>15\%$ on the stopout day. (Macro beta overrode alpha).
3. `INSTITUTIONAL_DISTRIBUTION`: Delivery % was low or Futures OI was unwinding during initial rally. (Retail bull trap).
4. `EARNINGS_SURPRISE`: Unscheduled corporate filing, credit downgrade, or sudden news. (Tighten corporate announcement filters).
5. `ENTRY_SLIPPAGE`: Entered outside recommended price band. Had user waited for optimal limit zone, trade would have been a WIN.
